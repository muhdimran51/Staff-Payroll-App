Staff Payroll

Online-only payroll · paste WhatsApp shifts · calculate exact hours · prepare payment summaries

Workflow: 1. Import shifts. 2. Check Driving payments if needed. 3. Open Paid records, load the month, and mark the monthly payroll Paid after payment. Individual shifts do not have paid/unpaid statuses.

Paste WhatsApp messages

Paste one or more messages. The app recognises dates, multiple time ranges, explicitly stated breaks, and delivery/collection counts. Breaks default to 0 unless written in the message. Unknown staff and duplicate entries are flagged for review.

Unpaid breaks default to 0 minutes. To deduct a break, include it in the message, for example: Aqilah: 9am - 6pm (30 min break).

Check the date, start/end times and break before saving. Overnight shifts are supported when the end time is earlier than the start time.

Delivery and collection counts are calculated separately. If no vehicle is specified, the app assumes Company Van. Company Van entries written as “trips” use S$5 per trip by default. Own Van entries should specify deliveries and collections separately so the correct rates can be applied. A line containing both a time range and trips creates an hourly shift and a separate driving entry. If no own-van label is present, Company Van is assumed. This app processes text you paste; it does not automatically read WhatsApp.

Driving payments

Delivery and collection payments are calculated separately by vehicle. Hourly wages (for example S$10/hour) still come from the employee’s timed shift record; trip payments are added on top.

Delivery / non-hourly entries

If “Own Van” is not stated, Company Van is assumed. Company Van counts are shown as trips and use the Company Van per-trip rate. For Own Van, specify delivery and collection counts separately; a message saying only “trips (Own Van)” must be broken down before it can be calculated.
